Information We Collect and Use

Our App requires certain permissions to provide its core functionality. We do not collect personal information like your name, email address, phone number, or any other information that directly identifies you.

  1. Location Permission (Approximate or Precise):

    • Purpose: Required only for the Compass feature to determine orientation accurately.
    • Data Collected: Location data is processed locally on your device solely for the compass functionality. We do not store your location history or transmit your location data to our servers or any third parties.

  2. Audio (Playback Only):

    • Purpose: Used solely to play the pre-recorded sound for the Siren feature.
    • Data Collected: The App does not access your microphone or record any audio.

  3. Device Information & Usage Data (Non-Personal):

    • Purpose: We may automatically collect strictly non-personal, aggregated information about your device (e.g., device model, operating system version) and how you interact with the App (e.g., features used, session duration) through standard tools like Google Play Console analytics. This helps us understand usage patterns, improve the App, and diagnose issues. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.
