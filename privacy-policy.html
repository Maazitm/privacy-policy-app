Privacy Policy for Power Flash SOS

Last Updated: October 2026

Thank you for using Power Flash SOS ("App"), developed by Siddi Furqan Maaz ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our App. Power Flash SOS is designed as a utility and does not require user accounts or collect personal identification information directly.

1. Information We Access and Process

Our App requires certain hardware and software permissions to provide core features. We do not collect personal identifying information like your name, email address, or phone number directly on our servers.

  1. Location Permission (Approximate or Precise):

    • Purpose: Used locally for two features: (1) calculating device heading and magnetic orientation for the Compass feature, and (2) populating current GPS coordinates into SMS or messaging drafts when you choose to use the emergency "Send My Location" broadcast feature.
    • Data Handling: Location data is processed strictly on your device. We do not track location history, nor do we store, log, or transmit your GPS coordinates to our servers or any unauthorized third parties.

  2. Flashlight & Hardware Controls:

    • Purpose: The App requires access to the camera hardware solely to control the physical LED light for Torch, SOS, Strobe, Blink, and Police Light functions.
    • Data Handling: The App does not access your camera lens, capture photos, or record video files.

  3. Audio (Playback Only):

    • Purpose: Used solely to play audio files for the Siren feature.
    • Data Handling: The App does not access your microphone or record any sound.

  4. App Performance & Diagnostic Data:

    • Purpose: We may collect non-personal diagnostic metrics (such as crash logs, app responsiveness, and operating system version) through standard tools like Google Play Console to diagnose technical bugs and maintain app stability.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing maintenance and feature development, the App displays mobile advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK).

Third-party advertising partners, including Google, may collect and process specific technical data to deliver, target, and measure ads, as well as to prevent invalid traffic and ad fraud:

  • Device & Other Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (GAID), device brand, model, operating system, and network operator.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse location derived from IP addresses to serve regionally relevant ads.
  • SDK Performance & Diagnostics: Ad impression counts, interaction metrics, and crash reporting.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services

You can manage, reset, or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time via: Settings > Google > Ads.

3. Google Play Services

The App integrates standard Google Play Services for system-level capabilities, including In-App Reviews and update checks. These interactions are governed directly by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data. Data is only handled through third-party SDKs (such as Google AdMob) as disclosed above, or when strictly required by applicable legal obligations.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Power Flash SOS operates without user accounts and does not store personal profiles on external cloud servers, we do not maintain personal user databases. Advertising identifiers can be reset or removed directly through your Android device settings.

6. Security

All network communications utilized by third-party advertising SDKs are encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated revision date.
